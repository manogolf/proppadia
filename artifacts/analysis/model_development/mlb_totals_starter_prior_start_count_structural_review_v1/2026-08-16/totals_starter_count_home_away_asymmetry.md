# Home/away structural asymmetry

Construction, cutoff, sparse threshold, fallback hierarchy, scaler magnitude, and cumulative-growth behavior are symmetric. Development means are home `18.737` and away `18.667`. Controlled within-pitcher outcome correlations are home `0.0298` and away `0.0423`.

The large repair coefficient asymmetry therefore has no corresponding governed construction asymmetry and is classified `LIKELY_ARTIFACT`, not a demonstrated baseball home/away effect.
