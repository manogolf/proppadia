# MLB Hits and Total Bases Research Archive

This archive separates completed MLB Hits and Total Bases research evidence from active production and daily operations. Packages remain intact, including negative, failed, inconclusive, and superseded experiments.

## Taxonomy

- `hits/`: Hits 0.5, Hits Over 1.5, Hits Under 1.5, and shared Hits studies.
- `total_bases/tb15/`: probability-model, UBO-5, BetOnline universe, batting-order, characterization, Under, overlap, reconciliation, and decommission evidence.
- `shared_research_foundations/`: historical population, starter, PA, feature, identity/outcome, market, and process foundations.
- `superseded_and_failed_experiments/`: preserved negative work.
- `indexes/`: chronological, topic, decision, and script lookup.
- `manifests/`: complete inventory, relocations, hashes, retained dependencies, and unresolved conservative holds.

UBO-5 is decommissioned; its live historical export root remains at `backend/mlb/exports/model_v2/ubo5_tb15/` pending final archival of postponed-game records. The model-independent active BetOnline board remains at `backend/mlb/exports/model_v2/bol_tb15/`.

Hits Under 1.5 remains available for future continuation. Archival placement is organizational and is not a rejection or decommission decision.

Use `indexes/chronological_index.csv` for date lookup, `indexes/topic_index.csv` for subject lookup, `indexes/experiment_decision_index.csv` for outcomes, and `manifests/relocation_map.csv` to translate former paths.
