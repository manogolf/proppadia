# Reconstruction design decision

`FULL_SEASON_COHERENT_DESIGN = FULL_COHERENT_RECONSTRUCTION_NOT_FEASIBLE`

The task's Phase 1 stop condition is met.

## Design A — exact artifact historical replay

The artifact is training-exposed through July 8, so a clean exact-artifact record can begin no earlier than July 9. Daily prepared feature files exist for much of July 9–August 13, but before August 3 they do not carry sufficient row-level snapshot/run/source timestamps to prove one governed strict-as-of state, and their population is market/candidate-derived rather than an independently reconstructed official eligible-hitter denominator. August 3–13 append-only lineage is exact prospective evidence already audited; reusing it is not a coherent 2026 reconstruction.

## Design B — chronological frozen-procedure reconstruction

The fitted procedure cannot be reproduced historically because the original training identities, source-branch environment, database snapshots, training-data hashes, and historical as-of feature rows were not retained. Rebuilding from today's mutable database would substitute hindsight-refreshed state and would not be the frozen historical procedure.

## Consequence

Neither design can honestly produce the requested season-to-date coherent record. No prediction ledger is frozen, no outcomes are attached, and no metrics, baseline comparisons, market comparisons, or certification inference are computed.
