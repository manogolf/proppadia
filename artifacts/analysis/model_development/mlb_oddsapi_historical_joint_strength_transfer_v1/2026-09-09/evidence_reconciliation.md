# Fixed 76-game evidence reconciliation

## Bottom line

The fixed cohort's economics transfer across the acquired books; its statistical explanation does not. The appropriate paired conclusions are **`ALTERNATIVE_BOOK_TRANSFER_SUPPORTED`** and **`MODEL_MARKET_AGREEMENT_DIRECTIONAL_BUT_UNRESOLVED`**.

The 76 unchanged selections went 56–20. Of 760 frozen bookmaker-game cells, 755 contain valid pregame prices and five are bookmaker absences. All nine acquired alternatives produced positive complete-eligible-coverage hypothetical flat-risk ROI; eight cover all 76 games and BetOnline covers 73. Identical-common-game comparisons with Pinnacle also remain positive at both prices. This supports two claims: the observed 56–20 win rate generated positive captured-price returns across books, and the return was not a Pinnacle-only pricing artifact. It does not establish user access, limits, fillability, or executable profit, and no book is preferred from observed ROI.

This is economic price transfer, not statistical confirmation. The 755 cells repeatedly price the same 76 outcomes, so they are correlated price observations rather than 755 independent games. Every bookmaker's date-clustered ROI interval includes zero. In the earlier decomposition, the model-after-market coefficient was negative in all five rolling folds and the combined forecast scored worse than market-only; in the later specification it was negative in four of five folds, while the market coefficient was positive in all five. The fixed agreement-indicator score differences also include zero, and the market-probability-matched ROI lift of +14.0 percentage points had a date-clustered interval of [-10.2%, +36.0%]. Market probability therefore remains the stronger demonstrated explanatory input.

The earlier analogue is weak. The sparse earlier-characterization model/market-strong-favorite slice covered 21 games, went 14–7, and returned about +1.4%. A related strictly prior historical strong-aligned/nonpositive-edge slice went 22–11 but returned -0.8%. Neither is an independent confirmation of the current effect.

## Precisely supported

1. **Supported:** the fixed cohort's observed win rate generated positive hypothetical returns at every acquired alternative book on each book's complete eligible coverage.
2. **Supported:** the economic sign survives identical-game comparisons, so it is not a Pinnacle-only price artifact.
3. **Unresolved:** agreement by the independent model has not demonstrated value beyond a market-only strong-favorite rule. Cross-book repricing cannot answer that conditional-information question.

## Smallest separating falsification test

Run one locked prospective risk-set test, not another bookmaker search. Before outcomes, enroll every market-strong side at the unchanged threshold and designated timestamp, retain the same side and a predeclared non-outcome-selected price source, and label whether the unchanged model also calls that side strong. Compare a market-probability-only forecast with that same forecast plus one fixed agreement indicator using blocked-date out-of-sample Brier/log loss; secondarily compare price-conditioned flat-risk returns with date-clustered uncertainty. Freeze the horizon from a pre-outcome power calculation based on a declared minimum meaningful lift and do not stop on observed ROI.

Classify **`MODEL_MARKET_AGREEMENT_VALUE`** only if the agreement indicator improves market-only out-of-sample scoring and the risk/price-adjusted agreement contrast clears its predeclared clustered interval. Classify **`MARKET_STRONG_FAVORITE_VALUE`** if the market-strong cohort remains directionally/economically positive but the agreement increment again fails. This single prospective contrast is the smallest design that changes the information axis while holding market strength, timing, side selection, and price policy fixed.

No cohort, model, outcome, threshold, production process, scheduler, publication surface, or wagering workflow was changed.
