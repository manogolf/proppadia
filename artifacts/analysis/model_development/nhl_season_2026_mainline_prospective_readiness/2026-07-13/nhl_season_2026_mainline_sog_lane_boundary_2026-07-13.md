# SOG Lane Boundary

The NHL player shots-on-goal lane remains independent. Mainline observation must not replace or disable SOG jobs, share player-prop candidate policies, merge player/game populations, reuse thresholds, or write into SOG recommendation/execution outputs.

Shared infrastructure is limited to official schedule/team identity, raw HTTP/odds transport patterns, timestamp utilities, immutable archive conventions, and generic hashing/health-check helpers. Mainline uses game-level `h2h`; SOG uses player-level proposition markets. Each lane keeps separate run IDs, schemas, health ledgers, grading populations, and governance decisions.
