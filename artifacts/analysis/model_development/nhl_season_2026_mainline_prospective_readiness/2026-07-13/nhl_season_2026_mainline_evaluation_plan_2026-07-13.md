# NHL Season 2026 Mainline Prospective Evaluation Plan

Evaluation is descriptive and population-specific. Population A supports accuracy, Brier score, log loss, ROC AUC, fixed-bucket calibration, ECE, mean probability, and observed home-win rate. Population B additionally supports champion probability minus proportional-de-vig market probability, model/market direction agreement, and movement from the earliest observed quote to the latest certified pre-puck-drop quote.

Population C characterizes results by projected, likely, confirmed, changed, and unknown goalie states. Population D characterizes capture quality and material lineup changes. These context analyses do not authorize feature fitting. Population E alone may later support execution-return calculations, and only from user-supplied execution price evidence. Market snapshots must never be treated as execution.

No betting threshold, candidate filter, ROI claim, recalibration, or champion retraining is defined here. Review prediction quality and capture completeness separately after a minimum of 200 graded Population A games and at least 60 calendar days of shadow observation; this is a review trigger, not a promotion threshold.
